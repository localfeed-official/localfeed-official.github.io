운영자는 SNS 계정 비밀번호를 받거나 저장하지 않습니다. 플랫폼별로 사용하는 권한은 아래뿐이며, 팔로워 목록이나 메시지 등은 읽지 않습니다.

  • TikTok: 기본 프로필 확인(user.info.basic), 초안 업로드(video.upload)
  • Instagram: 기본 프로필 확인(instagram_basic), 승인한 콘텐츠 게시(instagram_content_publish)
  • Threads: 기본 프로필 확인(threads_basic), 승인한 글 게시(threads_content_publish)

2. 이용 목적

  • 매장 홍보 콘텐츠 제작과 사장님 확인
  • 사장님이 승인한 콘텐츠를 연결된 SNS 계정에 게시하거나 초안으로 전송
  • 상담, 공지, 문의 답변

3. 보관 기간과 파기

  • 이용 계약이 끝나거나 사장님이 삭제를 요청하면 30일 안에 지웁니다.
  • SNS 접근 토큰은 해지 즉시 지웁니다.
  • 사장님 얼굴 사진과 목소리는 동의를 철회하면 즉시 지웁니다.
  • 법령에 따라 보관해야 하는 거래 기록은 정해진 기간 동안 보관합니다.
  • 전자 파일은 복구할 수 없는 방법으로 지우고, 종이 문서는 파쇄합니다.

Privacy Policy (English summary)

Effective date: November 24, 2026. LocalFeed is operated by Hibrick.

What we collect

  • Owner name, phone, email, shop name, address and business type.
  • Shop photos and shop information (prices, hours) provided by the owner.
  • The owner's face photo and optional voice recording, only with written consent, for owner videos.
  • For connected platforms: the account identifier and access tokens issued through official sign-in (for TikTok: open_id and tokens), and upload status.

We never collect or store social media passwords. For TikTok we request only user.info.basic and video.upload. We do not read followers, messages or other account data.

How we use it

To create content for the owner's shop, show it to the owner for approval, and upload approved videos to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts (or publish on other connected platforms as agreed).

Retention and deletion

Access tokens are deleted immediately when the owner disconnects or cancels. Face photos and voice recordings are deleted immediately when consent is withdrawn. Other data is deleted within 30 days after the contract ends or on request, except where law requires retention.

Processors and international transfer

We use Google (storage, email), Anthropic (AI writing assistance), Higgsfield AI (AI image and video generation), GitHub (web hosting and temporary image hosting) and the social platforms themselves. Data may be processed outside Korea, including in the United States.

Your rights

Owners can request access, correction or deletion at any time by email. TikTok access can also be revoked in the TikTok app settings.
